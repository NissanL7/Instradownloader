Instagram Video Downloader

Download Instagram Videos, Photos, Reels, IGTV & Carousels in original quality — fast, clean, and private.

How to download from Instagram?

Follow three simple steps to save public Instagram videos, Reels, photos, and carousels.

1

Copy the URL

Open the Instagram app or website and copy the link of the photo, video, Reel, carousel, or IGTV post.

2

Paste the link

Return to FastDL Alt, paste the URL into the input field above, and click the Download button.

3

Save your media

Choose your preferred quality option and save the file directly to your phone, tablet, or computer.

Address Bar Shortcut

Skip visiting the homepage by prefixing any public Instagram URL in your browser's address bar:

f-d.app/https://www.instagram.com/fastdl.app/reel/DO5tIDME6t-/

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about formats, limits, and privacy.

What formats and quality are supported?

Photos are saved as JPG (up to 1080 × 1350 px) and videos as MP4 (typically HD 720p or original upload quality) with no watermarks.

Do I need to log in or install software?

No. Everything runs in your browser with no account, no Instagram login, and no app installation required.

Can I download from private accounts?

No. Only public Instagram posts that are visible without logging in can be fetched.

Is link history stored on servers?

No. Links are processed in real time and not retained after delivery.
