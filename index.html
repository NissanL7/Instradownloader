How to download from Instagram?

STEP 01

Copy the URL

Open the Instagram app or website and copy the link of the photo, video, Reel, carousel, or IGTV post.

STEP 02

Paste the link

Paste the URL into the input field above and click Download. Our backend validates and resolves available formats.

STEP 03

Download

Pick your preferred quality option (MP4 or JPG) and save the file directly to your phone, tablet, or computer.

Frequently Asked Questions

What formats and quality are supported?

Photos are saved as JPG (up to 1080 × 1350 px) and videos as MP4 (typically HD 720p/1080p) with no watermarks.

Do I need to log in or install software?

No. Everything runs in your browser with no account, no Instagram login, and no app installation required.

Can I download from private accounts?

No. Only public Instagram posts visible without logging in can be fetched.

How do the backend functions work?

The Node.js backend exposes POST /api/validate, POST /api/resolve, GET /api/download, GET /api/languages, and GET /api/health, plus address-bar shortcut routing.
